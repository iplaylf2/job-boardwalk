Keep your place in research reports when opening source links.

HTTPS source links in Dashboard research reports now open in a new tab, leaving the report open at
your reading position while you consult the original source. A ↗ marker with a screen-reader label
identifies links that open in a new tab. Links to report sections and Dashboard pages continue to
open in the current tab.

This release also updates the desktop application's bundled runtime and dependencies.
